about=Gives the standard QGIS interface a Halloween theme (the Halloween skin of EnviroSolutions' MAIP application): a light variant with white and pale violet surfaces and a dark variant with deep violet surfaces, both with a pumpkin-orange accent and a dark menu bar decorated with animated pumpkins, bats and ghosts (the animation can be switched off). The layout of QGIS is not changed. Switching the skin off restores the previous style sheet and palette. English and Polish.

changelog=
    1.0 - First release: Halloween Light and Halloween Dark themes; a dark menu bar with pumpkins, bats and ghosts, animated as in MAIP (flying across the menu bar, in place, or off); the Nosifer face on the menu bar, pane titles and welcome-page headings; Halloween cursors for Pan, Select Features, Zoom In, Zoom Out and Identify; a spider web over EnviroSolutions plugin icons on hover; a spider easter egg (type halloween into the coordinate box and press Enter). The on/off switch, theme and animation mode are remembered.
